# Monument Bank: We're a bank that understands wealth means more than money. It's about the rewarding life it helps you live. 

> Monument is the first UK fully licensed bank designed to help professionals, entrepreneurs and ambitious savers grow and enjoy their wealth. Traditional banking treats everyone the same. Private banking requires millions. Monument is built for the people in between. Savings Account start with a £25,000 minimum deposit requirement. £10,000 for Easy Access Cash ISAs. Eligible deposits are Protected by the FSCS up to £120,000.
